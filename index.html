Christian's World

A whole island in 3D. Start at your house, then go anywhere: drive any car you find, take the speedboat out to Treasure Islet, or fly the helicopter over the city. Twenty-four missions: deliver pizzas, drive a taxi, win the Grand Prix, rescue kittens off a roof, dig up buried treasure.

Every mission pays out a tool or a blaster that you keep. A jetpack, rocket skates, a freeze ray, a thunder hammer. You'll need them, because the Professor's robots have escaped, and the biggest one is as tall as a house.

Open world Drive, sail and fly 24 missions 23 tools and blasters Day and night Works on phones and controllers

Zombie Gunsmith

You're on a ridge. Eight hundred metres out, a laboratory is burning — the place the outbreak started. They're coming out of the hole in the containment wall and walking straight at you. You have a Barrett .50 cal, a scope that goes to 16x, and as long as it takes to line up the shot.

Build the rifle first: optic, barrel, muzzle brake, bipod, ammunition. Every good part costs you something. Then hold your breath and start picking them off. The further out you drop one, the more it's worth.
